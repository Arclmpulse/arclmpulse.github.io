Quite possibly, the most ambitious FE game released until FE16.

Plot... aw man, I really hate to shit on it because it is my personal favourite Fire Emblem, but it was MESSY. Kicking off a few years in the future post-FE9 with Daein being a very poor country occupied by Begnion, Act 1 of the game focused on the Dawn Brigade, Sothe from FE9 (remember that little shit? haha) and his possible groomer and main character of the game, Micaiah (blah.) rising up the occupation forces and having Daein "take back their country" if you will. It was good, honestly. Act 2 pivoted to the now Queen Elincia from FE9 as she struggled to play politics and mind games in a country increasingly getting more agitated from Daein rising up as a force again as a conspiracy to have her throne usurped is what this short act was about. Again, pretty good. Act 3 focused on war breaking out between Begnion and the Laguz tribes as Daein and Crimea found themselves getting dragged into the thick of it, where we alternate playing as Ike leading the Laguz and Micaiah being forced to comply with Begnion as they blackmail the new King of Daein. And lastly, Act 4 shows the fallout of the war as characters on all sides of the conflict band together and try to take back their lands from the god who saw no purpose in keeping any life alive as they failed to keep their promise to never fight again for a millennia.

Yeah... I dunno. FE9 had a strong cast but I think they shoehorned way too many old characters in and didn't give the new ones much room to breathe. It's very telling that the longest segment of the game is Act 3, and you mostly play as Ike's army again there. And FE9 did such a great job of not having a higher power involved with only a mad king, only to have the events of the prior game be pre-determined by a higher power anyway.

Micaiah is actually a very fascinating character, grooming allegations aside (seriously she raised Sothe since he was a kid, WTF man???). The lost sister and true heir to the Begnion Empire, branded (half human, half heron in her case), and a wandering priestess who wanted to do good in Daein and help the common folk there. As someone who initially tried to use her powers for good, her arc really revolved around the idea that the road to hell can be paved with good intentions - like the river crossing or the mountain level in Act 3. Act 4 kinda devolves her character as she becomes a vessel to Yune as they try to stand up to Ashera, but the idea with her character was really interesting, and I find it a shame she got so little focus by the end of the game.

Ike... well I kinda have to mention him here as a main character too, right? I really hated seeing him get so much spotlight in this game. Yes he's a gigachad and so much better than Micaiah as a unit, but he's more or less... the same Ike. We do get his story arc resolution as it's revealed that General Zelgius from FE9 was the Black Knight who killed his father, and the 1v1 level in Act 4 against Zelgius is iconic but there's no real growth from him in this game, which is disappointing.

Villains... well, Senator Lekain can be argued as the main villain of the first three acts as a warmonger, racist, and the one who's mostly responsible for turning Daein into a hellhole and even gave them the illusion of choice as he manipulates Pelleas (new King of Daein) like a puppet and forces Daein into doing Begnion's bidding in Act 3. You could also argue that Sephiran from FE9 and the Prime Minister of Begnion who instigated most of the events of FE9/FE10 after losing faith in humanity and decided to awaken Ashera by plunging the continent of Tulius into war to reset the world - he failed in FE9 because the Dragons refused to join of course. And lastly, Ashera is the final big bad in Act 4 as Micaiah/Ike and co. band together to fight for their survival alongside Ashera's counterpart Yune. I think Lekain was fun as a villain though I think the blood contract plot device was really stupid, Sephiran being a mastermind was jaw dropping, and Ashera was boring, my personal take though.

Gameplay is pretty similar to FE9. Hard mode is a lot harder in this game and one of the hardest in the series, they take away showing enemy movement on your turn so you have to be so much more careful. I'd say this game is more balanced like the GBA games, mounted units make a triumphant comeback in this one (Haar is absolutely ridiculous by the way, there's a case he's one of the best units ever in the series), weapon triangle units aren't bad either. It's archers and mages that catch the short end of the stick here, only Shinon and Soren are really worth using.

And I have to say, it's not even funny how bad the Dawn Brigade (Act 1 gang) is in comparison to the Greil Mercs (most of Act 3). There's a real case that you pick none of the DB over the GM counterparts for the final battle.

This game has a few iconic maps. FE10 2-E (Elincia's Gambit) is my favourite level in the series, and it's the ultimate castle defense level. FE10 3-7 (Rivals Collide) is a really fun map and has one of the most battle conversations in the series. Felt like Captain America: Civil War when the Dawn Brigade and Greil Mercs fought. And one of the most difficult maps in the game, FE10 3-13 (Blood Contract) where the Dawn Brigade have to survive against the Greil Mercs in another castle defense is really fun.

OST was really strong too. The Devoted, Bearer of Hope, Eternal Bond and Ascent are all really great themes.

My gripes with the game are purely plot related. It really is one of the best Fire Emblems gameplay-wise and set up a lot of interesting levels from a game design standpoint. But perhaps the game got way too ambitious plot-wise and they let it off the rails when they decided Ike was more important than the literal main character of the game, lmao. It will always be my favourite FE game, but there are definite issues with it.
